🖼️ Task 1 — Edit Your Photos & Scans
Practice · 2–3 images is enough
Goal: Make your photos look clean. White paper should look white.

1 · Get Your Work onto the Computer

🖨️ Scan

  1. Put the page on the glass. Close the lid.
  2. Swipe your card → Scan to Email
  3. Choose 300 dpi
  4. Open the email. Save the file.

📸 Photograph

  1. Use daylight. No direct sun.
  2. Hold your phone straight above the page.
  3. Take a few photos. Choose the best one.

2 · Edit in Photoshop — click each step

1
Copy the Layer
▶
  1. Open your image.
  2. Press Ctrl/Cmd+J. Your original stays safe underneath.
2
Make It Brighter — Levels
▶
  1. Press Ctrl/Cmd+L
  2. Move the black and white sliders in to the edges of the graph. OK.
3
Add Contrast — Curves
▶
  1. Press Ctrl/Cmd+M
  2. Drag the line into a small S shape. OK.
4
Fix the Colour — Camera Raw
▶
  1. Filter → Camera Raw Filter
  2. Move Temperature and Tint until the paper looks white. OK.
5
Save + Export JPEG to Desktop
▶
  1. Save: Ctrl/Cmd+S → .PSD
  2. File → Export → Export As → Format: JPG → Export
  3. Choose Desktop. Name it, e.g. edit1.jpg. Save.

Colour Experiments — click each one

Start with Experiment 1. Then choose at least one more.

◐ = the half-circle button at the bottom of the Layers panel.
One object only? Select it first with W, then click ◐.
1
Colour Fill + Blend ModeColour one object
Start here
▶
  1. Object Selection Tool W — drag a box round the object
  2. Click ◐ → Solid Color. Pick a colour. OK.
  3. Change the blend mode (top of the Layers panel, says Normal)
  4. Lower the Opacity (top of the Layers panel) — try 40–70%.
Try:MultiplyOverlayColorDifference

⚡ Tip

  • New object? Press W and select again — then a new ◐ → Solid Color, different colour.
2
Gradient MapNew colours for the whole image
Easy
▶
  1. Click ◐ → Gradient Map
  2. In Properties, click the small arrow next to the gradient. Pick one.
  3. Lower the Opacity (top of the Layers panel) — try 40–70%.
3
Colour PopBlack and white — one object in colour
Easy
▶
Needs colour: use a colour photo, or do Experiment 1 first.
  1. Object Selection Tool W — drag a box round the object you want to keep in colour
  2. Flip the selection: Select → Inverse Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+I
  3. Click ◐ → Black & White. Everything except your object turns grey.
  4. Lower the Opacity for a softer grey — some colour shows through.
4
ColorizeTint the image one colour
Easy
▶
  1. Click ◐ → Hue/Saturation
  2. Tick Colorize. Move the Hue slider.
  3. Lower the Opacity (top of the Layers panel) — try 40–70%.
5
PosterizeFlat, graphic areas of colour
Easy
▶
  1. Click ◐ → Posterize
  2. Move the Levels slider. Fewer = flatter.
  3. Lower the Opacity (top of the Layers panel) — try 40–70%.
6
Halftone DotsBig print dots
Experimental
▶
  1. Copy the layer: Ctrl/Cmd+J
  2. Filter → Pixelate → Color Halftone → OK
  3. Lower the opacity so the drawing shows through.
7
Save Your Best
▶
  1. Save: Ctrl/Cmd+S → .PSD
  2. File → Export → Export As → Format: JPG → Export
  3. Choose Desktop. Name it, e.g. colour1.jpg. Save.

Make Your Page — click each step

1
Make a Background — Content-Aware Fill
▶
  1. Rectangular Marquee Tool M — select an interesting texture in your image
  2. Ctrl/Cmd+C then Ctrl/Cmd+V
  3. Hide the original layer: click the 👁 eye
  4. Magic Wand Tool — click the white area (it is in the same group as the Object Selection Tool W)
  5. Edit → Content-Aware Fill → OK
2
Make the Background Lighter
▶
  1. Merge the layers: Ctrl/Cmd+E
  2. Dodge Tool O · Range: Midtones · Exposure: 20–30%
  3. Use a big soft brush. Paint over the page.
Brush size: [ smaller · ] bigger
3
Add Your Images
▶
  1. File → Place Embedded → choose an image
  2. Resize: Ctrl/Cmd+T. Press Enter when done.
  3. Make your most important image the biggest. Make the others smaller.
4
Move Things a Little
▶
  1. Arrow keys move 1px
  2. Shift + Arrow moves 10px
5
Add a Soft Shadow
▶
  1. Click fx (bottom of the Layers panel) → Drop Shadow
  2. Opacity 30–50% · Distance 5–10px · Size 5–10px. OK.
6
Export Each Page — JPEG to Desktop
▶
  1. File → Export → Export As → Format: JPG → Export
  2. Choose Desktop. Name it firstname_lastname_page1.jpg. Save.
7
Make Your PDF
▶
  1. Open the PDF Builder
  2. Drop in your page JPEGs. Check the order.
  3. Add your name and student ID
  4. Click Download PDF. Open it and check it.
